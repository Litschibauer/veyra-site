VEYRAPRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Who we are

VEYRA is an independent project run by Tobi (“we”). Contact: veyracomputing@gmail.com.

What VEYRA Studio is

VEYRA Studio is our own software that produces the daily VEYRA video (gold and Bitcoin market brief) and publishes it to VEYRA's own channels on YouTube, Instagram and X. It is not a public service: it runs privately on our own computer, has no sign-up and no other users, and it only ever connects to VEYRA's own accounts.

YouTube API Services

VEYRA Studio uses YouTube API Services. With the permission of the channel owner (ourselves), given through Google's OAuth 2.0 sign-in, it

  • uploads VEYRA's own videos to VEYRA's own YouTube channel, with title, description, tags and the disclosure of synthetic content,
  • sets the custom thumbnail of those videos,
  • reads the public statistics of those videos (views, likes, comments).

It does not read, store or process data of other YouTube users, viewers, subscribers or comments. By using YouTube features, users are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What we store, where and for how long

  • Access token: the OAuth refresh token for VEYRA's own channel is stored as a local file on our own computer. It is never shared and never leaves our computer except in requests to Google.
  • Video records: the IDs, links and public statistics of VEYRA's own videos are stored in a local database on our own computer. Statistics are refreshed every six hours for 30 days after publishing; daily snapshots are deleted after at most 35 months.
  • We do not sell, rent or share any of this data with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

Cookies and tracking

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking. VEYRA Studio does not place or read cookies on anyone's device; the only data it keeps on a device is the access token and the video records described above, on our own computer.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access granted to VEYRA Studio can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. To have stored data deleted, write to veyracomputing@gmail.com; we delete it within 7 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
